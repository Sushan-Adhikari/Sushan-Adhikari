AI/ML researcher and Computer Engineering graduate (Kathmandu University, CGPA 3.90/4.0). I publish in **low-resource NLP, LLM alignment, retrieval-augmented generation, and explainable AI**, and I take ideas the whole way — from a first-authored paper to a system that runs in production.

### What I do

- **Research → production.** Low-resource NLP, LLM alignment, and RAG — published first, then deployed.
- **Legal & multilingual NLP.** Built the first public Nepali–English legal parallel corpus and a directional-asymmetry study of legal machine translation (ACM ICAIL 2026, first author).
- **Computer vision at scale.** Real-time inference with NVIDIA DeepStream and Triton — YOLOv8 across 10+ simultaneous camera streams at ~60 fps (Computer Vision Intern → Junior MLOps Developer at KyraWorks).
- **Work that ships.** Co-founded Nurvexa (RAG-based medical exam prep, deployed) and integrated anti-spoofing liveness checks into Nepal's national e-pension platform (300K+ users).
